**New section, placed under the first answer box — "Buying from the USA: what is different"**

> **Time zones.** Ahmedabad is 9.5 to 13.5 hours ahead of the US, depending on your time zone and the season. We schedule walkthroughs and calls for your evening, which is our morning, so nothing waits a day.
>
> **Signing from the US.** If you can't be in Ahmedabad for registration, a power of attorney lets a trusted person sign for you. It can be executed in the US and made valid in India through an apostille or at an Indian consulate. We coordinate the wording with a local lawyer so the document is accepted by the sub-registrar.
>
> **Payment route.** Payments come from your NRE or NRO account in India, or through an inward remittance in your name. Your bank or a chartered accountant should confirm the route for your case, including any tax treatment in the US and India. We do not give tax advice.
>
> **Seeing the home without flying.** We shortlist two or three homes, walk each one on live video, send the title and RERA details, and only then ask you to decide. Many US families visit once, to sign or to see the finished home.
>
> **After you buy.** Handover, snagging, furnishing and, if you wish, rental or caretaking, so the home isn't empty while you're abroad.

**Two FAQ entries to add**
- *Can I buy a home in Ahmedabad without travelling from the USA?* — Yes, if the paperwork is arranged in advance: a valid power of attorney, identity and address documents, and an NRE/NRO account. We coordinate each step and tell you what must be done in person.
- *Do I need a US tax advisor as well?* — Usually yes. Income or gains from Indian property can be reported in both countries, so speak to a chartered accountant or a US tax professional who knows India. We can refer you.
